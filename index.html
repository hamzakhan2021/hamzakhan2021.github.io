Something I built end to end

Client work stays under NDA, so here is a product I own outright. A live walkthrough is available on a call.

Own product · designed and built solo

CiteSignal measures whether AI engines name your business.

It asks ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity the questions your buyers ask, records who gets named and which pages get cited, and turns that into a fix list. A public site runs a free crawler scan in about 20 seconds; paid reports run the full measurement.

  • Passwordless accounts, credits and online payments with signed webhooks
  • Audit engine sampling every question three times, so one lucky answer can't skew a score
  • Lead capture with a review dashboard, and "Book a call" scheduling through Cal.com
  • Structured data on every page, automated backups, and deploys that never drop a running job

Node.js · PostgreSQL / Supabase · Paddle · Docker · Cloudflare · LLM APIs
